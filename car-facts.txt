## The training car (from its window sticker)

Vehicle: 2027 Audi Q7 quattro. This is the all-new third-generation Q7. Merapi Gray metallic, black interior, built in Bratislava, Slovakia.

Window sticker: Base MSRP $70,900. Total MSRP $85,315, including the $1,395 destination charge.

Options on this car:

- Premium Plus package, $4,400

- Executive package, $2,600

- 21-inch Black optic package, $1,750

- Vision package, $1,600

- Trailer hitch, $850

- Merapi Gray metallic paint, $595

- Audi Beam rings, $550

- Audi Guard protection kit, $410

- Wheel lock kit, $150

- Audi cargo box, $115

Powertrain: 2.9-liter twin-turbo V6 with 429 horsepower and 442 lb-ft of torque. 8-speed Tiptronic transmission with quattro all-wheel drive. 0 to 60 in 4.8 seconds, which Audi calls best in class. Tows up to 7,700 pounds with the hitch, and this car has the hitch.

Fuel economy (sticker): 16 city, 23 highway, 19 combined.

Seating: Seven seats. The second and third rows fold under power, and every second- and third-row seat has LATCH child-seat anchors.

### Standard on this car

- 14.5-inch center touchscreen, 12.3-inch passenger display, and Audi virtual cockpit plus

- MMI Navigation plus, Apple CarPlay and Android Auto, and two wireless phone charging pads

- Leather seats. Heated, power front seats with memory and lumbar.

- Panoramic sunroof (upgraded by the Executive package, below), three-zone climate, and wood inlays

- LED headlights with high beam assist, and illuminated front and rear Audi rings

- Power tailgate, Audi advanced key, and remote start through the myAudi app (subscription)

- Surround-view cameras with front, side, and rear parking sensors

- Automatic emergency braking, blind-spot warning with exit warning and rear cross-traffic assist, lane departure warning, adaptive cruise, traffic sign recognition, and 9 airbags

- Built-in toll module, which works with E-ZPass-style tolling (subscription)

The driver monitoring camera makes sure the driver's eyes stay on the road. It needs cell service and GPS, and it comes as a limited-time trial. [VERIFY trial length and the cost after the trial]

- Bang & Olufsen Premium sound with 3D sound, which includes speakers that add height to the music

### Executive package ($2,600)

- Dual-pane acoustic glass in the side windows, for a quieter cabin

- Four-zone automatic climate control, so the back seats get their own settings

- Heated third-row seats

- Panoramic roof with variable light control: the glass switches between clear and frosted

- Power rear side-window sunshades

### 21-inch Black optic package ($1,750)

21-inch 5-V-spoke two-tone wheels with all-season run-flat tires. Black exterior trim and roof rails, dark Audi rings, and dark chrome exhaust tips.

### Vision package ($1,600)

Digital Matrix LED headlights. Each headlight has tens of thousands of micro-LEDs that shape the high beam around other cars, so you can keep your high beams on without blinding anyone. Audi says this is the first time the technology is offered in the US. The package also includes a head-up display that puts speed and navigation on the windshield, and headlight washers.

### Accessories on this car

Audi Beam rings project the four Audi rings onto the ground when a door opens, which looks great at night. The Audi Guard kit adds protective mats and liners [VERIFY exact contents]. The car also has a wheel lock kit and a cargo box.

### Warranty and maintenance

4-year/50,000-mile new vehicle limited warranty, 12-year corrosion perforation warranty, and 4 years of roadside assistance. Audi Signature Care covers scheduled maintenance at 1 year/10,000, 2 years/20,000, and 3 years/30,000 miles.

## What's new in this generation

- 94 more horsepower than the last Q7's V6 (429 versus 335), and about seven-tenths of a second quicker to 60

- Maximum cargo space of 78.1 cubic feet, about 10 more than before. 43.9 behind the second row and 15.1 behind the third.

- Taller, flatter roofline for more headroom in the back rows

- Three screens: an 11.9-inch driver display, a 14.5-inch center screen, and a 12.3-inch passenger screen

- USB-C charging up to 100 watts in all three rows

## Lineup and step-up options

2027 Q7 lineup, starting MSRP before destination: Q7 Premium $70,900. Premium Plus $75,300. Prestige $84,900. SQ7 $98,700. This car is a Premium with the Premium Plus package plus the Executive and Vision packages, so it already has most of the Prestige content. Building value in what's on this car comes first.

- Prestige. Adds adaptive air suspension, sport seats plus with massage, and Remote Park Assist Pro, which can park the car while you stand outside it. Teach consultants to bring it up when a customer mentions ride comfort, a sore back, long road trips, or a tight garage.

- SQ7. 4.0-liter twin-turbo V8 with 591 horsepower and 590 lb-ft, 0 to 60 in 3.7 seconds. Standard air suspension, rear-biased quattro, and a locking rear differential. Teach consultants to bring it up when a customer loves the acceleration or says they wish it had more.

- Bang & Olufsen 4D Premium sound ($950). 22 speakers and 1,360 watts, plus seat actuators that let you feel the bass. Teach consultants to bring it up when a customer loves the stereo.

- Six-seat captain's chairs ($450). A first for the Q7, with an aisle to walk through to the third row. Bring it up if kids fight over the back or adults ride in the third row. [VERIFY folding details]

- Luxury package. [VERIFY contents and price.] Don't describe it; tell consultants to check the order guide.

Any of these would mean a different car or a factory order. Consultants should check timing and pricing in the store systems, never promise either on a drive.

## Competitors (facts only, always polite)

- BMW X5: No third row in the US. The Q7 has seven seats standard, more maximum cargo space, and a quicker 0 to 60 time.

- Mercedes GLE 350: 255 horsepower against the Q7's 429. The GLE's third row is an extra-cost option, while it's standard on the Q7.

- Volvo XC90, Genesis GV80, Lincoln Aviator, Lexus TX, BMW X7, and Mercedes GLS: Don't quote their specs. Teach consultants to ask what the customer liked about it and connect that to Q7 strengths.
